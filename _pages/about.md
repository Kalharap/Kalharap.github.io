I'm a final-year PhD candidate in the Department of Physics and Astronomy at the University of Nevada, Las Vegas, working on hydroclimatology, paleoclimatology, and hydrology. I expect to graduate in May 2027.

My research tries to understand how streamflow in the Upper Colorado River Basin has varied over centuries, and what that long record tells us about present-day drought.

## Research interests

- Tree-ring based streamflow reconstruction and dendrochronology
- 21st-century Colorado River drought in a long-term context
- Statistical and machine-learning methods for hydroclimate (Gaussian processes, Bayesian inference, deep learning for tree-ring measurement)

## News

- **Dec 2026**: Presenting two dissertation chapters at AGU 2026, and serving as an early-career convener for a session.
